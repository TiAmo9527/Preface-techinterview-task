# Specification

## Product intent

Executives and room managers need one room data-management and analysis system to understand each room's facilities, acquisition costs, depreciation, and expected replacement cycle, alongside the condition of its lighting, water supply, and air conditioning. The system should help them identify issues, prioritise maintenance, and plan replacement spending across properties.

## Input

- A small fictional dataset covering properties in four locations.
- Sample invoices and separate spreadsheet tables, combined into linked property, room, and asset records.

## Output

- Import data, view individual rooms, and add or edit asset records.
- Record facility type, purchase and installation dates, cost and currency, useful life, depreciation, remaining value, and expected replacement date.
- State calculation and currency assumptions.
- Provide a portfolio overview with filters for location, hotel, and room.
- Provide a room-level view of lighting, water supply, and air-conditioning status.
- Allow managers to log faults, assign an owner, and update maintenance progress.
